PROMISSORY NOTE

Principal Amount: $50,000.00
Date: March 1, 2026
Maturity Date: March 1, 2027

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, Borrower promises to pay to the order of Lender the principal sum of Fifty Thousand Dollars ($50,000.00) together with interest at the rate of 6.5% per annum.

Section 1. Payment Schedule
Payments shall be made in equal monthly installments of $4,315.00 on the first day of each calendar month.

Section 2. Prepayment
Borrower may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time without penalty or premium.

Section 3. Default and Acceleration
If Borrower fails to make any payment when due, and such failure continues for ten (10) days, Lender may declare the entire unpaid principal balance immediately due and payable.

Section 4. Governing Law
This Note shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
