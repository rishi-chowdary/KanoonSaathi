How it works

  1. Upload a contract. PDF, DOCX, and plain text files up to 10 MB are accepted. Scanned PDF pages fall back to Tesseract OCR.
  2. Extract and analyze locally. The app segments clauses by exact character offsets, classifies them with local models and keyword rules, pulls out dates and payment figures, and flags common one-sided terms.
  3. Review, ask questions, or export. Switch between simple, detailed, and structured clause explanations, ask page-cited questions in document Q&A, or download a 10-section PDF report.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Not legal advice. Risk warnings and missing-clause notes are informational only and do not replace a lawyer.
  • Extractive summaries. Summaries select verbatim sentences from the contract rather than rewriting from scratch, so wording stays close to the source.
  • Rule-based simplification. Plain-language rewrites rely on a glossary and sentence-splitting rules, which can read less smoothly than human paraphrasing.
  • Runs on your machine. No contract text is sent to any external API; English documents only, and DOCX page numbers are approximate.
